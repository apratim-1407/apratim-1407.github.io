Approach
Hard problems, done properly.

Four habits I hold myself to, whether it's a one-page teardown or a shipped feature.

  1. 01

    Work comes first

    I show up prepared, own the outcome, and finish what I start.

  2. 02

    Hard is good

    The difficult problem is usually the valuable one. I go toward it, not around it.

  3. 03

    Past the first answer

    If the first approach doesn't hold up, I find another, then check it again.

  4. 04

    Attention to detail

    Hand-label the data, read the 1-star review, test the edge case. Small things decide outcomes.
